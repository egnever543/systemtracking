The Problem

Do you know exactly where
each conversion comes from?

Most media buyers don't. And that's costing them dearly.

The pixel can't see WhatsApp

When the lead clicks your ad and goes to WhatsApp, the pixel loses track. The conversion isn't attributed. Your ROAS looks lower than it really is.

Multiple groups turn into chaos

Manually distributing leads across full groups, with no idea which ones are packed, and no way to track which ad each lead came from.

The checkout never gets the fbclid

When you redirect to an external page, the ad parameters vanish along the way. Attribution breaks entirely.

How MeuFluxo works

Set up in minutes. Track forever.

01

Install the snippet

One line of code on your site. MeuFluxo starts capturing fbclid, gclid and UTMs automatically.

02

Set up your destinations

WhatsApp, groups, checkouts, Telegram. Set distribution weights. Choose your funnel type: direct, pre-sale, form or VSL.

03

Each click gets a unique ID

WA-AB3X7K — traceable back to the originating ad.

04

Conversion logged → CAPI fires

Facebook, Google and TikTok receive an enriched event with all the data from the original click.

Everything a media buyer needs

No workarounds. No manual integrations.

Weighted routing

Define what % of leads go to each destination. Automatic load balancing on every click.

Full CAPI

Facebook, Google Ads and TikTok. Lead event on click, Purchase/conversion when you log the sale.

Parameters preserved

Custom funnels

Pre-sale page, lead capture form or YouTube VSL before the redirect.

Client portal

Webhooks + UTM Analytics
